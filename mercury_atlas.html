Reading this map & method

Scope: career only. The ratings, assessments and transit interpretations in this atlas concern career. They are not overall life ratings or assessments of other life domains.

Colors and numbers represent the supplied dasha rating, from 1 (max leverage) to 5 (max caution). Transit tone is separate and never changes the cell color. Filters dim nonmatching cells without moving periods. Each AD has its own dated timeline, with PDs ordered from earliest to latest and sized by their actual duration. Rows have different date ranges, so vertically aligned positions are not the same calendar date. Tap or inspect a PD for exact dates; previous/next follows the full chronological sequence.

Mercury MD dates derive from the Open Ephemeris-verified Moon at 17°06′ Libra (Swati p4), using a 365.2425-day Vimshottari year. Transit snapshots are calculated at each PD midpoint with Swiss Ephemeris 2.10.03 in Lahiri sidereal mode; three key checkpoints (Jul 2026, Sep 2027, Mar 2032) were cross-verified against Open Ephemeris. Houses shown are whole-sign houses from Scorpio Lagna. “Transit tone” is an interpretive overlay, not a probability or empirical forecast.

This atlas preserves the supplied assessments and precomputed transit snapshots. It does not recalculate charts or apply the archived engine’s calibration.
